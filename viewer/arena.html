ABOUT THIS EXPERIMENT

A crowd laboratory, with clear boundaries.

The arena uses independent behavioural controllers. Each agent has its own position, velocity, response delay and route choice. Local avoidance and contact dynamics determine the resulting movement. Fly icons represent circular agents scaled for the venue.

The MaleCNS connectome experiment remains in the separate brain lab. The moving flies here are not copies of one neural output, and are not presented as full connectome simulations.

Repellent transport, contact forces and venue geometry are simplified models. They do not establish human evacuation safety, crowd injury risk or biological fidelity.

Exports include the seed, configuration, controller description and measured results so runs can be inspected and repeated.
